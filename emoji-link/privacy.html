Onet Connect: Animal Match – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Onet Connect: Animal Match ("the app") is a free puzzle game made by an independent developer. This policy explains what information is handled when you play.

Information the app stores

Your game progress (current level, scores, number of hints/shuffles and the sound setting) is stored only on your device. The app has no account system, no server, and the developer does not collect, receive or sell any personal information.

Advertising

The app is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data. This processing is performed by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from Ad choices on the app's home screen. On iOS, the app asks for permission before accessing your device's advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency); if you decline, you will still see non-personalized ads. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

Rewarded ads

Some features (extra hints, shuffles or time) can be unlocked by voluntarily watching a video ad. Watching is always optional, and the app never asks you to pay.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes all game data stored on your device. For data processed by Google for advertising, see the Google links above.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
