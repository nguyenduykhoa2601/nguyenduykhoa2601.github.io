Snake Hunt – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Snake Hunt ("the app") is a free, single-player arcade game made by an independent developer. You play offline against computer-controlled snakes. This policy explains what information is handled when you play.

Information the app stores

Your game progress – best length, kills and times, coins, unlocked skins, daily gift streak and your settings (sound, vibration, reduce motion, language) – is stored only on your device. The app has no account system, no server and no analytics, and the developer does not collect, receive or sell any personal information.

Advertising

The app is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data. This processing is performed by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings › Ad choices in the app. On iOS, the app asks for permission before accessing your device's advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency); if you decline, you will still see non-personalized ads. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

When you play the browser version on CrazyGames, ads are provided by CrazyGames and its partners under the CrazyGames privacy policy; the game itself still keeps your progress only in your browser.

Rewarded ads

Some extras – a second chance after you are eaten, doubled coins, a doubled daily gift, and a few skins – can be unlocked by voluntarily watching a video ad. Watching is always optional. The app contains no in-app purchases and never asks you to pay.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules one local reminder per day (for your daily gift) directly on your device. No push service or server is involved and no data leaves your device for this. You can turn it off any time in your device settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app (or clearing the site data in your browser) deletes all game data. For data processed for advertising, see the Google and CrazyGames links above.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
